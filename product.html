How to Use

Four simple steps

1

Open & unwrap

Open the individual wrapper and remove the panty liner.

2

Peel

Peel off the release paper from the adhesive side.

3

Stick

Place the adhesive side onto the centre of your underwear and press gently to secure it.

4

Wear & change

Wear throughout the day according to your comfort. After use, remove and dispose of it responsibly.

How to Dispose

Please dispose responsibly

1

Remove

Remove the liner from your underwear.

2

Roll or fold

Roll or fold it inward.

3

Wrap

Wrap it using the individual wrapper, tissue or suitable paper.

4

Bin it

Place it in a waste bin. Do not flush used panty liners down the toilet.

Please Dispose Responsibly — Keep Your City Clean.
